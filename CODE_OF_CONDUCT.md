# Code of conduct

HeatBox follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: everyone who takes part in this project's issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to be respectful and considerate, to accept that others see things differently, to give and take criticism of the work gracefully, and to keep to the subject. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexual language or attention, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not keep to this, and may block someone who repeatedly ignores it.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here makes this a worse place to be, tell the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile ([vDeimos](https://github.com/vDeimos)). Reports are read by the maintainer only and are kept in confidence.

## Scope

This applies in the project's own spaces, and when someone speaks for the project elsewhere.
